# Independent review — OPP-004 Visual proposal studio for remodelers (2026-10-27)
Reviewer: the seat that disagrees. Read-only; no contact, sign-ups or spend.

## 1. Evidence check
- Cooper's 86/100 is a discovery-stage score. His own 2026-09-24 scorecard dropped it to 27/50 (Demand 5, Competition 4, Economics 4). Those September labels are honest: it says there are no interviews, no payments and no revenue. The 86 at the top of the list is out of date and shouldn't be used to rank OPP-004 first.
- The cited files aren't on the box: FOUNDER_FIT_OPPORTUNITY_RESET_2026-08-17.md, OPP-004_COMPETITOR_MAP, the pain-evidence ledger and OPP-004_MANUAL_PAID_EXPERIMENT. No archive file matches "OPP-004", and the pack has no OPP-004 URLs to open, so I couldn't check the sources behind the claims.
- Software prices (Chief Architect, ProKitchen) are vendor list prices, labeled FACT. They are fine as price anchors, but they show remodelers pay for software, not for this service.
- The "pain" evidence comes mostly from homeowner forum posts and one creator's time report. That is attention from the wrong buyer, not payment from remodelers.

## 2. Premise challenge — strongest contrary evidence (VERIFIED, fetched today, vendor pages)
- **Fast Kitchen Design (FKD):** flat $100 per room with 2020 Flex design, photoreal renders, a LiveSpace 3D walkthrough, plans and elevations, by 9 AM the next day, with unlimited revisions. Render-only is $50. Volume packs go down to $75 per room. It claims a 30+ designer team, 500+ businesses and 15K designs a year. (fastkitchendesign.com/outsource-kitchen-design)
- **247 Cabinetry:** $29.99 per kitchen, or $1,399 a month for 60 designs, delivered in 24 hours, with the first design free. (247cabinetry.com blog, 2026-10-08)
- **Boutique studios:** about $599–$1,199 per kitchen still (sayodesigns.com). Software alternatives include Cedreo (free tier plus paid plans), 2020, Chief and Houzz Pro.
- **What this means:** paying demand exists, but it is already met by cheap, fast, offshore-scale services. Cooper's planned $249 offer costs 2.5x FKD and promises one revision against FKD's unlimited.
- **Hidden assumptions:** that visuals are the sales bottleneck; that a branded proposal wrapper is worth paying a premium for; that one person can match overnight turnaround.
- **Opportunity cost:** this is a labor-bound service, which is the opposite of the low-maintenance, high-profit-per-hour product company Matteo wants.
- **3 most likely failure reasons:**
  1. Price is set by the $30–$100 incumbents, so the hourly rate collapses.
  2. Without calls or social media, there's no way to reach remodelers who already have a vendor.
  3. Revision and turnaround load turns it into a 24/7 job.

## 4. Verdict: **KILL as currently framed** (manual visual-proposal studio)
Gates 2, 3 and 5 fail against named, cheaper rivals. If Matteo wants to keep the theme, re-scope it to a *software* product that undercuts the services on cost-per-proposal. That would be a new opportunity and would need a fresh scoring.

**One 7-day check before fully closing it, if Matteo wants one:** publish a single offer page for a specialty that FKD/247 don't serve, e.g. exterior/whole-home photoreal concept proposals from phone photos, priced at $149. List it on one existing marketplace or directory. Count only paid orders or $50 refundable deposits.
- PASS: 3 or more paid orders or deposits within 7 days.
- FAIL: 2 or fewer means KILL.

It needs no calls or spend. It does need Matteo's approval to create a Stripe payment link and an account or listing on the chosen marketplace, and to publish the page.
